Let the glow hear the music

Browsers don't let a page hear YouTube or Spotify directly, so on its own the glow follows the player: playing, pausing, the volume and every new record. To make it move with the actual sound, let Vinyl listen.

Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. The sound is only measured, on this device, while this is on. Tap the same button to stop.

For this tab, pick This tab and keep Share tab audio on. The microphone works when the music plays out loud, not on headphones.
